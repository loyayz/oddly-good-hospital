# Door-query local references

Retain a small equivalent local-work reduction under the user's policy, not a demonstrated whole-game acceleration or CPU-target pass.

Base: `3ffb9c83b77588cf5944dbae8cd5e285bcdf4fdb`. The only production change replaces a temporary three-field `entry` Dictionary in `HospitalSpace.movement_doors` with two references to the same `BodyIndex` and `door_order` Dictionary. Geometry acquisition, live reservation queries, candidate ordering, returned live door references, query counters and cache/LRU call order remain unchanged. No new cache or collision bypass, business scheduling change, rendering change, workload reduction or weaker threshold.

## Evidence and scope

The full-load diagnostic found 677520/705754 ordinary movement door queries empty (96%). It still executes both the door index and current reservation query. Diagnostic clocks are not production cost and total spans must not be added to child spans.

Four real-state bounded warm microbenchmark strata, alternating original/candidate eight batches, show median local query reductions:
- Actor body, empty result: 8.8105 to 8.3115 microseconds (-5.66%).
- Actor body, nonempty: 10.7765 to 9.9965 (-7.24%).
- Derived single-person floor-step query, empty: 9.349 to 8.7845 (-6.04%).
- Derived floor-step query, nonempty: 11.986 to 11.048 (-7.83%).
These are query inputs derived from real facts, not executed movement. Bounded samples are not frequency weighted; do not average these percentages into a whole-game gain. Returned values, order and door Dictionary identity match, with World facts unchanged. Internal runtime caches are not part of World.freeze.

Additional twelve cold/warm/borrowed-geometry query comparisons cover multiple doors, member reach/order and a reservation-only additional door. Independent cache snapshots agree on source/geometry keys, LRU recency, charge and query counts; borrowed geometry does not touch navigation recency. The additional reservation is generated by the existing giant-convoy fixture's formal group move and one real movement tick, producing a span-six door reservation. The subsequent small query rectangle is a calculation boundary, not proof its querying actor crossed the door. The initial span-two fixture could not produce this boundary and is retained as a failed prerequisite, not a production regression.

## Full workload: new ABBAAB comparison

Three controls and three candidates ran in order O1 C1 C2 O2 O3 C3, using the same initial 160 actors, 1000 prefix and 6000 measured steps, unchanged comprehensive operations and acceptance thresholds. Every run passes business coverage and exact final World/RNG/ordered-event comparison: `8baf906509e5d0bfd5266adcfe5a3455fb274cc3aae00e786a6996b46d15dbbb`. All six raw rounds are separately persisted and actually read back member by member; the latest three candidate originals are retained.

The dense qualified window has 5439 samples at indices 1000–6438, population at least 144. Control to candidate three-round medians:
- P95: 123.582 to 120.216 ms (-2.72%).
- P99: 151.745 to 155.943 ms (+2.77%).
- Maximum: 437.701 to 451.600 ms (+3.18%).
- Mean: 64.903805 to 66.314167 ms (+2.17%).
- Sum of all 6000 measured step spans, excluding prefix: 379.639619 to 385.524376 seconds (+1.55%).

Results are mixed: lower P95, but higher P99, maximum, mean and aggregate time. The retention decision values the simple local reduction and treats the observed overall increase as not large; it does not claim causal global improvement, prove zero regression, or meet the absolute CPU targets. ABBAAB is not randomized; environment variation and historical unguarded/failed process terminal-state uncertainty remain despite cooperative locking of all current runs. See the companion JSON for each round.
